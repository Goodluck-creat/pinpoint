How it works

Three steps from "package created" to "customer sees it arrive."

01

Business creates the delivery

Enter the customer's details, pickup and destination, and assign it to a rider — right from the dashboard, no paperwork.

02

Rider accepts and shares location

The rider approves the delivery and, once the customer asks to track it, approves that too — tracking never starts without consent.

03

Customer watches it happen

A live map shows the rider moving toward them in real time, with an alert if the route ever drifts off course.

Why PinPoint
